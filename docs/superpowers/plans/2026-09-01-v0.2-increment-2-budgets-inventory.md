**Goal:** Close the Workshop Core money-and-parts loop: versioned quotes (labor, parts, taxes, discounts) with partial authorization evidence, and an inventory ledger (products, append-only movements, derived stock) with reservation/consumption tied to service orders.

**Scope (FR coverage):**

- `FR-SVC-007` quote with labor lines, part lines, taxes and discounts; deterministic totals.
- `FR-SVC-008` quote versioning: revisions never overwrite; supersession is explicit.
- `FR-SVC-009` authorization / rejection / partial authorization with recorded evidence (actor, timestamp, per-line decisions).
- `FR-SVC-011` reserve and consume inventory against order task lines.
- `FR-INV-001` (basic) products with SKU and unit; `FR-INV-003` every stock change is an `InventoryMovement`; `FR-INV-004` stock is derived from the ledger, never stored as mutable truth.

**Out of scope here:** purchase orders (FR-INV-008), physical counts (FR-INV-010), providers (FR-INV-007), POS (FR-POS-*), payments, attachments/photos, QR.

**Key domain decisions to enforce:**

- **Ledger immutability:** `inventory_movements` is append-only (like `service_order_events`); corrections are compensating movements, never updates. Stock = `SUM(quantity)` filtered by product/location; there is no mutable stock column.
- **Reservation discipline:** `RESERVE` movements hold stock (quantity negative against available); `RELEASE` compensates a reservation; `CONSUME` finalizes it. Reservations reference the order; consumption references both order and reservation movement when applicable.
- **Quote versioning:** a quote belongs to an order; revisions append with `version = previous + 1` and `superseded_at` on the predecessor; totals are computed server-side from lines (never trusted from the client); money uses integer minor units (cents) with explicit currency, no floats.
- **Authorization evidence:** order gains `authorization` state data (decision, actor, timestamp, whole-line subsets for partials; monetary overrides require a quote revision); `authorize`/`reject`/`authorize_partial` are explicit actions with audit events in the order timeline.
- **Synchronization and commands:** Products retain their independently qualified offline-first synchronization. Initial quote create/revise/decision operations use the accepted bounded online-command boundary; offline drafts are local work, not automatic financial mutation replay. Inventory movements remain server-authoritative online commands.
