# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its behavioral baseline.

Project spaces must remain respectful, inclusive, accessible, and free from harassment, dehumanization, discriminatory conduct, and abuse of authority.

## Enforcement

Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers through the security/contact channel published by the project rather than opened as public issues. A maintainer with a material conflict of interest must recuse themselves from enforcement decisions.

Reference: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
