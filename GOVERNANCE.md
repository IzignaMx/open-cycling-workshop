# Governance

The project begins in a bootstrap-maintainer phase and is designed to transition toward shared governance as contributor diversity grows.

## Decision mechanisms

- **ADR** for material technical architecture decisions
- **RFC** for cross-cutting product, protocol, ecosystem, or governance changes
- **Constitutional RFC** for license, governance constitution, trust model, or project-mission changes

Accepted ADRs and the newest normative specification version take precedence over older narrative documentation.

## Release authority

Stable releases require at least two authorized maintainers. No person or sponsor may purchase Verified/Official plugin status or a merge decision.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers must disclose and recuse themselves from material conflicts involving vendors, customers, paid plugins, enforcement, security embargoes, or commercial relationships.
